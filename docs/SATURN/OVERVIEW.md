# 🌌 Project Saturn: The Philosophical Foundation

Project Saturn is more than a cleanup; it is the implementation of **Chronological Order** within a digital workspace. In a world of rapid AI iteration, the greatest risk is not a lack of capability, but the accumulation of "digital sediment"—deprecated versions, orphaned stubs, and fragmented logic.

## The Core Tenet: "Essence Over Volume"
The ModMind Agentic Stack operates on the principle that a lean, well-mapped system outperforms a bloated one. Saturn ensures that every file in the root directory serves a primary purpose, and every experimental lab in the outer rings has a clear path to either **Integration** (moving to core) or **Obsolescence** (moving to archive).

## The Architecture of the Rings
1. **The Nucleus**: `thoth_orchestrator` (The point of singularity and command).
2. **The Inner Ring**: The ModMind Stack (The revenue and execution pipeline).
3. **The Outer Rings**: `labs/` (The space for mutation, failure, and discovery).
4. **The Event Horizon**: `archive/` (Where entropy is neutralized and preserved).

By maintaining this concentric structure, we ensure that experimental chaos in the labs never destabilizes the operational core.
