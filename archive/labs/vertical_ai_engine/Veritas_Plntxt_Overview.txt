Automated FOIA Feedback Loop
A cornerstone of Veritas AI's proactive approach is its automated FOIA Feedback Loop. This system intelligently processes responses to Freedom of Information Act requests, identifying patterns of deception and denial, and refining future requests.

How it Works:
📥
Ingest Response
Automated intake of FOIA denial letters and responses.

🔍
Analyze & Detect
AI analyzes text for patterns of deception, obfuscation, or unjustified denials.

🔄
Refine & Re-Engage
Learns from denials to generate more effective follow-up requests.

The FOIA Feedback Loop is 100% Engaged, actively processing and learning. However, a critical area for refinement is the "detect_bs" algorithm.
