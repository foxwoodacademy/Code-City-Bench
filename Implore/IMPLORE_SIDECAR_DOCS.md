# Implore: Grant Writing & Societal Impact Engine

## Overview
Implore is an outwardly reaching tool designed to bridge the gap between technical, medical, commercial, societal, and educational projects and the funding necessary to realize them equitably.

## Key Capabilities (Enhanced)
- **Domain-Agnostic Generation:** Narrative generation adapts based on domain (e.g., medical, societal, educational) to frame projects as human-centric solutions.
- **Dynamic Ingestion:** Ingests research papers, problem statements, and community impact reports beyond just codebase scanning.
- **Broad Search Scope:** Searches federal and regional grants using broadened, multi-keyword scope (e.g., "equitable", "community", "impact").

## Future Roadmap & Vision
We are expanding into several high-impact areas:
1. **Digital Art Preservation:** Grants for long-term preservation of digital cultural artifacts.
2. **Human-Model Apprenticeship:** Mentorship paradigms where humans learn from AI/Model interactions.
3. **Physical-Digital Trade Mastery:** Replicating fine-motor skills and trade mastery through haptics, FEP (Field-Emission Processing), and advanced digital-physical synthesis.

*Goal: To drive systemic, equitable progress with or without federal funding.*
