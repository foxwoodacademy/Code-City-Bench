# Legal Roadmap: Dual-Entity Framework (Kansas)

## Objective
Establish a sustainable, grant-ready vessel for FEP/LOUGH and Implore projects, optimized for Kansas jurisdiction.

## Phase 1: Commercial Vessel (Kansas LLC)
1.  **Draft Articles of Organization:** File with the Kansas Secretary of State.
2.  **Operating Agreement:** Define roles, responsibilities, and FEP ownership.
3.  **EIN:** Obtain Employer Identification Number from the IRS.

## Phase 2: Societal/Non-Profit Vessel (501(c)(3) Entity)
1.  **Draft Articles of Incorporation (Non-Profit):** Incorporate in Kansas.
2.  **Bylaws:** Define the non-profit's mission (equitable societal impact).
3.  **IRS Application:** File Form 1023 (or 1023-EZ for faster approval).

## Phase 3: Integration
- Ensure clear contractual separation between the Commercial entity (IP holder) and Non-profit entity (Grant recipient).
