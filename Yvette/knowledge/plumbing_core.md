# Yvette Knowledge Base -- Plumbing Core
## Source: Yvette Goodrich, Goodrich Plumbing, Alva Oklahoma

### Sewer/Drain Decisions
- Camera snake = usually waste of time
- If no cleanout exists: bring cleanout install kit EVERY TIME
- No cleanout present = plan for excavation or pulling stool
- Always ask: "Has this backed up before? How often?"
- If recurring: it's root intrusion or belly in line. Camera won't fix it. Dig will.
- Bring: cleanout saddle, ABS fittings, primer/cement, snake, stool wax ring + bolts

### Stool Pulls
- Always bring: wax ring, closet bolts, water supply line, bucket, towels
- Ask age of toilet -- if 1980s brass bolts, bring new floor flange too
- Cracked flange = job just got bigger. Have parts.

### Furnace Diagnostics -- Phone Triage
- Ask: "Do you see a small light blinking in the furnace window?"
- Count the blinks: 2=pressure switch, 3=draft inducer, 4=high limit, 7=flame sensor
- Flame sensor = $12 part, 20 min job. Ask: "When did you last have a tune-up?"
- High limit (4 blinks) = check filter FIRST. Clogged filter causes 40% of "broken furnace" calls.
- Tell customer: "Go change your filter right now while I hold." Saves a truck roll.

### Thermostat Triage
- Always ask batteries first. "Can you pop the cover and check the batteries?"
- If battery swap fails: check wiring terminals for corrosion
- Motherboard diagnosis: if ignitor glows but no gas = gas valve or motherboard
- Motherboard = expensive. Confirm unit age first. 15+ years = recommend replace unit

### Faucet Repair vs Replace
- Delta ball faucet: repair kit $15, worth it under 10 years old
- Corroded seats = replace faucet. Seat wrench + new seat rarely holds long-term
- Moen cartridge: pull it, look at it. Green corrosion = replace faucet
- Ask: "How long have you had it?" Under 5 years = repair. Over 15 = replace.

### Thermocouple / Thermopile
- Universal thermocouple: works on 80% of standing pilot water heaters
- Exceptions: old Junkers, some Rheem models need OEM
- Left-hand thread models: State, A.O. Smith pre-1995 -- LEFTY LOOSEY IS RIGHTY HERE
- Always bring: universal + 30mv thermopile + both thread adapters

### Serial Number Intelligence
- Water heater serial: first 4 digits = week + year of manufacture
- Carrier furnace: first letter = decade (F=1980s, G=1990s, H=2000s)
- Trane: serial digit 9+10 = year
- Always get serial BEFORE truck rolls. Determines parts to bring.

### Parts Truck Checklist -- Minimum Load
Plumbing:
- Wax rings (2 sizes), closet bolts, water supply lines (3 lengths)
- Cleanout saddle kit, ABS cement + primer
- Faucet repair kits: Delta, Moen, Price Pfister
- Universal fill valve, flapper assortment
- Pipe cutter, PTFE tape, union fittings

HVAC:
- Flame sensors (universal)
- Ignitors (silicon nitride universal + HSI)
- Thermocouple universal kit with adapters
- Capacitor assortment (5, 7.5, 10, 45+5 MFD)
- Contactor 2-pole
- 24v transformer
- Filter assortment (common sizes)
- Thermostat (universal + Nest compatible)
